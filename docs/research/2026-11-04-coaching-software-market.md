## CoachPlus (Italian leader in the report)

- Full vertical SaaS for personal trainers, online coaches, studios: clients,
  programs, video library, load history and progressions, nutrition plans and
  macros, check-ins and questionnaires, measurements and photos, chat, agenda
  and booking, subscriptions and payments (Stripe, PayPal, Klarna).
- 2026: data from Apple Health, Google Fit/Health Connect, Garmin, Fitbit, Oura,
  Cronometer. AI assistant announced as "coming", not shipped.
- Reported traction: 3,000+ professionals, 50,000+ Play downloads; App Store
  Italy 4.2/5 (86 ratings).
- B2B: the coach pays, the client app is free. €19.90 / 15 clients, €49.90 / 50,
  €69.90 / 100, €99.90 / 300 (branded iOS/Android app), Enterprise above.
- No public API, webhooks or Zapier found (not specified, not necessarily absent).
- **Weak point: in-gym mobile UX.** Reviews ask for a rest timer that keeps
  running outside the app (lock screen, Live Activities), a less dense workout
  screen and compact supersets; a 2026 redesign made timer, notes and history
  harder to reach mid-session.
- Play Store data-safety says data "cannot be deleted": a GDPR question for a
  product holding health data and photos.

## What the report concludes

- The Italian market is crowded and prices are being compressed: more value at
  the same €10–25 entry price every few months.
- A new entrant should not rebuild CoachPlus for less, but differentiate on:
  an excellent mobile workout UX, automation of the coach's work, and
  interoperability / data ownership.
- Timing: European fitness 2025 at 75.5 M members and €39.1 bn revenue; ACSM
  2026 trends rank wearables 1st, mobile fitness apps 4th, data-driven tech 8th.
